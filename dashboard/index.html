Privacy, Cookies & Terms of Use

1. Essential-Only Cookies (Zero Tracking or Ads)

This hub uses strictly necessary first-party HttpOnly security cookies and local UI preferences — never advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies:

  • z340_clearance — Stores anti-bot verification clearance (up to 30 days) so you can access hub apps seamlessly.
  • astra_session — Optional login session token (up to 30 days) created only if you sign in.
  • Local Browser Storage — Stores optional client-side preferences such as RGB background colors and audio mute settings on your device only.

2. Minimal Data Stored & Self-Service Erasure

  • Optional Accounts: If you register, we store only your chosen username, a salted scrypt password hash, account timestamps, and submitted game high scores.
  • No Raw IP Database Storage: Transient rate-limiting counters run in volatile server memory to prevent brute-force abuse and are never persisted to the user database.
  • One-Click Right to Erasure: Signed-in users can change their password or permanently delete their account, sessions, and all associated leaderboard scores at any time via Account → Delete Account & My Scores.

3. Public Leaderboard Conduct Rules

Submitted usernames and scores appear on public leaderboards. Usernames containing profanity, slurs, URLs or spam domains, impersonation of staff/system roles, or automated score tampering are blocked and may be removed without notice.

4. Educational & Entertainment "As-Is" Disclaimer

All simulators, chemistry/astronomy tools, circuits, and arcade games on Astra 13's Swiss Knife Hub are interactive digital simulations provided strictly "as-is" without warranty of any kind for entertainment and educational exploration. Never attempt real-world chemical or physical experiments based on web simulations.
